Fontaines D.C. released their fifth album, *Dopamine Chamber*, last Friday! I listened to *Romance* and *Skinty Fia* (their previous albums) a good amount in 2025, mostly because the group was in the lineup of a festival I was going to. I thought they were an OK band, maybe a 7.5/10. Some boring lows but pretty sick highs. I was somewhat excited for the new album, but I listened to it today and I'm not impressed unfortunately. My previous impression of them was that they were often at their best with their more high-energy, fast paced songs, while their slower, atmospheric *shoegaze*-like stuff felt repetitive and uninteresting. And I like *shoegaze*; in fact *Sundowner* from *Romance* is a good example of them doing this style well. It's just that usually they don't hit it for me. Well, the thing is, this album is almost entirely entirely in this style... while it was playing, I felt like I was listeing to a constant drone of repetitive riffs while their singer went off about whatever it is that he goes on about in every song, but it always felt the same.

Okay this is a bit harsh, but I will listen to it again, and it's very possible that my opinion will change. Also, I'll acknowledge that *Marianne* and the closing track *Intimate* were pretty good, and Rosalía makes a cameo which is very unexpected and kinda cool.
