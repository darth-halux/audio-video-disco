I listen to music a lot, and I think a lot about it too. One of the things I've noticed is that looking back, every year has a distinct "musical theme" for me, which is essentially the type of music I remember being into at that time. For example, 2024 was the year I listened to a lot of contemporary music music (stuff that had been released that very year), 2023 was probably my heavy metal year, etc. As the current year of 2026 is coming to an end, it's becoming increasingly obvious that my musical theme for this year will be Jazz.

I've always listened to some jazz here and there, but this year was the first time I stopped to listen to entire albums, and in some cases, whole discographies of artists and groups I particularly liked. I was also lucky to get some crucial recommendations from friends that exposed me to specific subgenres that I thoroughly enjoyed. This means I'm starting to get a clearer picture of the genre, in all its awesomeness.

I was thinking today, and the main aspect of jazz I enjoy is how much of a **ride** it is. Every song is like hopping on a train whose destination you don't know, and appreciating both the moments when it's predictably going straight and the ones where it's swerving like crazy. While the greatest solos can still feel unpredictable and fresh even after many listens, the first few listens are truly magical. It's an experience that can be almost euphoric, as the artist essentially asks you to close your eyes, hold their hand and trust that they're taking you somewhere awesome.

I will write more about the specific songs, albums and artists I've been enjoying in the future. For now, I still need to let some ideas settle.
