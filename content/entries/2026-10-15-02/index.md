I don't consider myself to be a very creative person. I can't draw, make music, sing... writing is the only artistic endeavour I'm half decent at. One aspect of it that I do kinda pride myself upon is coming up with names. And for this website, I'm particularly proud of the name I chose.

Audio video disco is latin for "I hear, I see, I learn". That's it. The "hear" part I like because I plan on talking about music quite a bit on this blog; "see" because, well, you're seeing this stuff right now, and "learn" because hopefully I'll share some cool knowledge and fun facts.

Fun fact, the latin word *disco* doesn't have anything to do with disco music. The infinitive form is *discere* (to learn), where words like "disciple" or "discipline" come from. Disco music, on the other hand, comes from music discs, which in turn come from the greek *dískos*, a flat, circular object. Fun fact over, that's your daily dose of *disco*. Still, I appreciate the connection, because disco music is cool, and also Disco Elysium is one of the best games ever made.

Audio, Video, Disco is also the name of the second studio album by the great French electronic music duo Justice, released in 2011. I think that's where I first saw the expression, even though I haven't listened to that particular album. Their first album (untitled but often referred to as "Cross") is amazing though. Strong recommend to anyone who enjoys electronic music, specially French house/electro.

It's neat there if you see, I gave you a bit of learning and then a bit about music. Really honouring the name of the website you know. I suppose now to really tie it together, I should have an example of something you can see. Well, maybe the real *video* is the friends we made along the way.
