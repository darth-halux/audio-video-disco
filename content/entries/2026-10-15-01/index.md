This is the first post I'm making on this website. I created it because sometimes I have thoughts and observations that I think are interesting, or that I at least would like to keep written down for the future. 

I may also talk about music, games, movies or other media that I find interesting. Or fun facts that I've come upon.

I also created it to serve somewhat as a diary or journal. Since it's public, I won't be writing about stuff that's very personal, but it should stil be fun to look at in the future and remember what was once on my mind.

I never did anything in web development before, so I didn't know anything about HMTL, CSS, or any of that stuff. I thought about learning about all that, but to be honest I got a little bit lazy, so the backend for this website was largely coded by AI. I'm disclosing it because some people are against that. I just wanted to get it running so I could start writing, to be frank, and asking ChatGPT was the fastest way to do that. The actual posts won't have any of that of course.
