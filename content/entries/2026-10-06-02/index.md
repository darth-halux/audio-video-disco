title: "On leaving things unfinished"
preview: "Some thoughts need somewhere to sit for a while."
---

I used to think a notebook was somewhere to put things once I had worked them out. A place for the sentence after all the crossing out.

But most of what I want to keep is still uncertain. A conversation I am turning over. Something I noticed on a walk. A question that seemed small until I tried to answer it.

There is a particular kind of relief in writing something down without having to finish thinking about it. The page remembers it, and I can go and do something else.

Looking back through old notes, I sometimes find that the unfinished ones have more to give me. I can remember where I was sitting, or what I was trying to say. Sometimes I disagree with them. Sometimes the missing sentence arrives months later.

I would like to leave room for that here.
